What it does

Built for a life split across borders

Germany and India come built in. Each country is a readable pack of currencies, tax deadlines, calculators and document types, and you can add your own.

EUR · INR · USD…

Where you stand

Accounts, assets and debts in every currency, converted at ECB rates or your own. See each country's share and how your net worth moves.

Daily to yearly

Money in and out

Log income, spending and transfers once, or set them to repeat. Monthly charts show where it went.

PDF · Excel · Word

Documents that file themselves

Drop a payslip, bank statement or tax form. The rules read it, name it and suggest the folder; you confirm the move.

DE · IN

Tax deadlines and calculators

Deadlines that follow from your answers, a German refund estimate, India's old vs new regime, 1% TDS on property instalments.

Instalments

Big purchases, stage by stage

Track a flat under construction: what's paid, what's left with tax, the next instalment, and the documents for each stage.

Goals · practice

Plan and learn

Inflation-aware goals, a budget, and investing practice with backtests and paper trades. No real trading, ever.

How it decides

Rules first. AI only if you want it.

Sorting documents and creating deadlines is done by rules written in plain YAML: same input, same result, every time. The Rules screen shows which rule matched and why.

  • No AIEverything works: rules, calculators, deadlines, reports.
  • ClaudeAnthropic API key, or later through Claude Desktop on your plan.
  • Azure OpenAIYour own Azure deployment and key.
  • GitHub ModelsFree within limits with a GitHub account.
  • OllamaRuns on your computer, so nothing leaves it.
# your rules/my-rules.yaml — checked before the country packs
document_rules:
  - id: my_gym_invoice
    label: Gym membership invoice
    category: Receipts & bills
    match:
      text_any: [fitnessfirst, mcfit]
      filename_any: [gym]
    extract:
      - {name: invoice_no, pattern: "Rechnungsnummer\s*:?\s*(\w+)"}
    route:
      folder: "{folder:DE}/Receipts/{first_year}"
      filename: "{first_date}_gym_{invoice_no}{ext}"

deadlines:
  - id: my_car_insurance
    title: "Renew car insurance ({year})"
    due: "{year}-11-30"
Privacy and security

Your data stays in one folder you choose

Back up the folder and you've backed up everything. Delete it and it's gone. There's no server of ours to trust.

Read exactly what is stored and sent →
  • Local onlyThe app answers only its own page on this computer. Other websites and other devices are refused.
  • Keys in the keychainAPI keys go to Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain, never to the browser.
  • Links checkedRecords can't point at things that don't exist; upgrades back up first and never delete data.
  • Hide amountsAlt+H masks every figure when you share your screen.
  • Open codeMIT licence, tests on Windows, macOS and Linux, and code scanning on every change.
